# Code of Conduct

Omni-Grid welcomes contributors of all experience levels, including students
and people making their first open-source contribution. Participation in this
repository, its issues, pull requests, and project-related community spaces is
covered by this Code of Conduct.

This policy is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate of different backgrounds,
  identities, experience, and viewpoints.
- Give feedback about ideas and code, not a contributor's worth or identity.
- Assume good intent where reasonable, ask clarifying questions, and accept
  corrections gracefully.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and make room for first-time
  contributors to learn.
- Protect privacy. Do not post private messages, credentials, personal data,
  or real civic-complaint evidence without authorization.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, sexualized attention,
doxxing, deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, or publishing
someone else's private information are not acceptable. Unwelcome conduct
based on a protected or personal characteristic is not acceptable. Deliberate
attempts to expose credentials, sensitive civic data, or private reports are
also out of scope for this community.

## Reporting and moderation

For a conduct concern, contact the repository maintainers through a private
contact channel they make available on GitHub. This repository does not publish
a dedicated Code of Conduct email address or confidential reporting form. Do
not put sensitive details in a public issue or pull request. If no private
channel is available to you, do not publish the details; you may ask publicly
for a private reporting route without identifying the people involved.

Maintainers will review reports with discretion and share information only
with people who need it to assess or address the concern. They may remove or
hide inappropriate content, request changes, close discussions, or restrict
participation where repository tools permit. A response timeline is not
guaranteed.

## Scope

This Code applies in repository spaces and when someone is representing the
Omni-Grid project in a related public space. It does not replace GitHub's own
terms or policies. For security vulnerabilities, use the private disclosure
guidance in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not a public conduct or bug report.
